# Integrity and Knowledge Hierarchy — Ontology Proposal

**Status:** Proposed — documentation only; not approved as normative or implemented.

This document translates the canonical GAIA-Documentation proposal into repository-specific requirements for review. It does not override approved decisions, create implementation evidence, authorize new capabilities, or change runtime behavior. Reconcile it with existing contracts and issues before adoption.

## Proposed concepts

- **Reality / truth:** what is the case, independent of whether GAIA knows it.
- **Data:** recorded observations or symbols with origin and limitations.
- **Information:** data organized or contextualized for interpretation.
- **Evidence:** observations, records, sources, measurements, or results relevant to a claim.
- **Knowledge claim:** a proposition supported by specified evidence and methods, with known limitations.
- **Confidence:** a fallible, calibrated assessment of uncertainty; not a guarantee of truth.
- **Truth-seeking:** a repeatable process of corroboration, contradiction search, falsification, and revision.
- **Understanding:** an explanatory model of relationships, mechanisms, context, and limitations.
- **Wisdom:** context-sensitive judgment about consequences, trade-offs, timing, and appropriate action.
- **Stewardship:** accountable care for shared resources, people, systems, ecosystems, and future options.
- **Flourishing:** sustained conditions supporting life and communities to learn, adapt, cooperate, create, and thrive.

## Proposed relationships

Data → may be organized as → Information.
Information → may support → Knowledge Claims.
Evidence → supports or challenges → Knowledge Claims.
Confidence → qualifies → Knowledge Claims.
Truth-Seeking → evaluates and revises → Knowledge Claims.
Understanding → models → Relationships, Mechanisms, and Context.
Wisdom → informs → Decisions under uncertainty and competing values.
Stewardship → constrains and guides → Actions affecting shared systems and future options.
Flourishing → proposed beneficial outcome → subject to plural measures and explicit trade-offs.

These are semantic proposals, not a strict linear law. Concepts may be revisited, coexist, and be revised as evidence changes. Truth-seeking is a process; a confidence score or source reputation must not be treated as truth itself.

## Ontology review constraints

- Reconcile with existing human/AI, meta-knowledge, skills, capabilities, and knowledge graph taxonomies.
- Avoid equating truth, evidence, confidence, knowledge, understanding, and wisdom.
- Define provenance, temporal validity, source reliability, contradiction, and correction semantics before schema adoption.
- Avoid one universal scalar for flourishing; represent affected groups, dimensions, uncertainty, and trade-offs.
- Do not create machine-readable production schemas until approved contracts and ownership are established.
